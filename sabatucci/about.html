Who We Are

Based in Wallingford, CT, Sabatucci Construction is a family-owned and operated company. For over 30 years, we have been providing the community with top-notch carpentry and structural services. We built our reputation on a foundation of trust, transparent communication, and unparalleled craftsmanship.

Why Choose Us?

  • Local to CT: Deeply rooted in Wallingford and surrounding communities.
  • Decades of Mastery: 30+ years of dedicated professional carpentry.
  • Licensed & Insured: Full protection and proper code compliance for all jobs big and small.
  • Family Values: We treat your home like our own. Trustworthy and reliable.

Our specialization spans across kitchen remodels, outdoor decks, and everything in between. Whether you are looking for a complete overhaul or a beautiful new addition, Sabatucci Construction is ready to bring your vision to life.
