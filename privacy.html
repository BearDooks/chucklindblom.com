1. Introduction

Welcome to chucklindblom.com. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information when you visit my website.

2. Information We Collect

My website uses Google Analytics to help me understand how visitors engage with the site. Google Analytics collects information anonymously. It reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.

Google Analytics may collect the following data:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL (the page you visited previously)
  • Hostname of the accessing computer (IP address)
  • Time of the server inquiry

3. Cookies

This website uses cookies to function properly and to analyze traffic. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g., remembering your cookie consent preference).
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to track visitor usage. You can opt out of these by declining cookies in the banner or using a browser add-on.

4. How We Use Information

I use the data collected to:

  • Monitor and analyze the performance of my website.
  • Improve user experience and content.
  • Detect and prevent technical issues.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. Since Google Analytics data is anonymized, I cannot directly identify you to fulfill these requests based solely on analytics data. However, you can prevent Google Analytics from using your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
